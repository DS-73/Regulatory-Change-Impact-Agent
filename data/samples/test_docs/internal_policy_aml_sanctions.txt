Sanctions Screening and AML Transaction Monitoring Policy
Document: POL-AML-08 · Owner: Compliance · Approved by the Board Risk Committee, June 2026
[Internal Policy Document for RegWatch Compliance & Impact Mapping Test]

1. Purpose and Scope
1.1 This Policy defines the mandatory standards for real-time customer sanctions screening, PEP identification, and automated transaction monitoring across all domestic and cross-border banking operations.

2. Sanctions Screening Standards
2.1 The Bank shall screen all cross-border wire transfers and remittances in real time against the UN Security Council Consolidated List, OFAC, and local regulatory sanctions lists prior to transaction execution.
2.2 Domestic interbank transactions exceeding 50,000 in local currency shall be subject to post-transaction automated batch screening within 24 hours of settlement.
2.3 Any true match against a designated terrorist or sanctions entity must result in immediate blocking of the transaction and freezing of the associated funds without prior notice to the customer.

3. Suspicious Activity Reporting
3.1 Financial Crime Compliance must complete investigation and file a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) with the national Financial Intelligence Unit within 48 hours of internal alert escalation.
3.2 Employees are strictly prohibited from tipping off any customer or third party regarding the filing of a suspicious transaction report or internal investigation.

4. Data Retention and Audit Trail
4.1 The Compliance Department shall retain all customer due diligence files, screening audit logs, and transaction monitoring alerts for a minimum period of 5 years following account closure.
